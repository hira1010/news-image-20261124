## 5. COP31 climate talks collapse

The COP31 UN climate conference in Antalya, Türkiye, ended on Sunday 22 November with no overarching deal, after the EU and others rejected a final text that made no mention of moving away from fossil fuels — the first COP to end without an agreement since 2009.
